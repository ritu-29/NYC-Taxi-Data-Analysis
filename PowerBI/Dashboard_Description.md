## 📊 Project Overview

This project features an interactive **Power BI Dashboard** that analyzes New York City taxi trip data from **January to March 2026**.

The dashboard provides deep insights into:

* Revenue generation
* Trip distributions
* Passenger behavior
* Payment preferences

## 📊 Dashboard Visualizations & Insights

### 1️⃣ Financial Trends & Payment Preferences

**Revenue Trend**

* Tracks daily revenue fluctuations
* Helps identify peak business days and demand shifts

**Payment Breakdown**

* Credit cards dominate with **87.46% (~6.7M trips)**
* Cash accounts for **11.43% (~0.88M trips)**
* Indicates strong preference for digital payments

---

### 2️⃣ Operational Metrics & Demand Patterns

**Trips by Pickup Hour**

* Shows a classic commuter demand curve
* Lowest activity around **4 AM**
* Peak demand between **3 PM – 7 PM**
* Highest volume ~**0.55M trips** during peak hour

**Passenger Distribution**

* Single-passenger trips dominate (**~6.4M trips**)
* Group travel significantly lower

---

### 3️⃣ Trip Profiles & Correlation

**Trip Distance Distribution**

* Majority of trips fall within **0–5 miles**
* Indicates short-distance urban commuting behavior

**Distance vs Fare (Scatter Plot)**

* Strong positive correlation between distance and fare
* Helps identify:

  * Outliers
  * High-fare anomalies
  * Surcharge/toll impacts

## KPI Definitions

The dashboard includes the following calculated metrics:

Total Trips – Total number of taxi trips analyzed
Total Revenue – Total fare generated
Total Tips – Total tip amount collected
Average Fare – Average fare per trip
Average Distance – Average trip distance
Average Duration – Average trip duration
Average Passengers – Average number of passengers per trip
Average Speed – Trip efficiency (distance ÷ duration)
Revenue per Trip – Average revenue generated per trip
Revenue per Passenger – Revenue generated per passenger
Tip % – Percentage of tips relative to total revenue
Long Trip % – Percentage of trips classified as long-distance

## 🎛️ Filter Capabilities (Slicers)

The dashboard includes interactive filters for dynamic analysis:

* **Pickup Month** → January, February, March 2026
* **Payment Type** → Credit Card, Cash, Dispute, No Charge
* **Passenger Count** → Individual vs group travel
* **Pickup Hour** → Hourly demand analysis
