# 🚖 NYC Taxi Power BI Dashboard (Q1 2026)

## 📊 Overview

This Power BI dashboard provides an interactive analysis of NYC Yellow Taxi data for the first quarter of 2026 (January–March). It highlights revenue performance, trip patterns, passenger behavior, and payment trends using dynamic visualizations and KPIs.

---

## 📈 Key Metrics

| Metric            |           Value |
| ----------------- | --------------: |
| **Total Revenue** | $220.10 Million |
| **Total Trips**   |    7.57 Million |
| **Average Fare**  |          $19.54 |
| **Average Tip %** |          18.50% |

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 💳 Payment Preferences

* Credit cards dominate with **87.46% (6.62M trips)**
* Cash accounts for **11.42% (0.87M trips)**

### ⏰ Peak Demand

* Demand increases throughout the day
* Peaks around **7:00 PM (19:00)** with ~**0.55M trips**

### 👤 Passenger Behavior

* Most trips involve **single passengers**
* Trip volume decreases significantly for groups larger than 2

### 📏 Distance & Revenue

* Average trip distance: **3.42 miles**
* Majority of trips fall within **0–5 miles (~4.4M trips)**
* Strong positive correlation between **distance and fare**
* Some outliers exist due to tolls and surcharges

## 📊 Dashboard Features

* KPI cards for revenue, trips, fare, and tips
* Time-based analysis (Trips by Pickup Hour)
* Payment type distribution
* Passenger count analysis
* Trip distance distribution
* Scatter plot (Distance vs Fare)

---

## 📐 KPI Definitions

The dashboard includes the following calculated metrics:

* **Total Trips** – Total number of taxi trips analyzed

* **Total Revenue** – Total fare generated

* **Total Tips** – Total tip amount collected

* **Average Fare** – Average fare per trip

* **Average Distance** – Average trip distance

* **Average Duration** – Average trip duration

* **Average Passengers** – Average number of passengers per trip

* **Average Speed** – Trip efficiency (distance ÷ duration)

* **Revenue per Trip** – Average revenue generated per trip

* **Revenue per Passenger** – Revenue generated per passenger

* **Tip %** – Percentage of tips relative to total revenue

* **Long Trip %** – Percentage of trips classified as long-distance

---

## 🎛️ Filters & Interactivity

* **Pickup Month** → Compare Jan, Feb, March performance
* **Payment Type** → Analyze Credit Card vs Cash trends
* **Passenger Count** → Study individual vs group travel
* **Pickup Hour** → Explore time-based demand patterns
